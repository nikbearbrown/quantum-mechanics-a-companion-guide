# About Quantum Mechanics A Companion Guide

GitHub repository: [quantum-mechanics-a-companion-guide](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/quantum-mechanics-a-companion-guide)

Author: Nik Bear Brown

## Book Summary

`quantum-mechanics-a-companion-guide` is organized as a chapter-based book project. The chapters below outline the book's main instructional arc and source files.

## Chapter Summary

1. **Introduction** (`chapters/00-introduction.md`) - A learner opens the first chapter of *Quantum Mechanics A Companion Guide* with a familiar problem: there is too much information and not enough structure.
2. **Chapter 1 — Why Quantum Mechanics?** (`chapters/01-why-quantum-mechanics.md`) - - The Theory a Universe Built by Breaking Every Prediction Classical Physics Made.
3. **Chapter 2 — Mathematical Foundations** (`chapters/02-mathematical-foundations.md`) - - The language quantum mechanics is written in, and why no simpler language works.
4. **Chapter 3 — The Schrödinger Equation** (`chapters/03-the-schrodinger-equation.md`) - - The dynamical law of quantum mechanics was guessed, not proved.
5. **Chapter 4 — One-Dimensional Problems** (`chapters/04-one-dimensional-problems.md`) - - Why the Bottom Rung Is Never on the Floor.
6. **Chapter 5 — Quantum Formalism** (`chapters/05-quantum-formalism.md`) - - Two students sit in a library with two papers.
7. **Chapter 6 — The Hydrogen Atom** (`chapters/06-the-hydrogen-atom.md`) - - One electron, one proton, a Coulomb potential — and the cleanest test we have of whether quantum mechanics is right about the world.
8. **Chapter 7 — Angular Momentum** (`chapters/07-angular-momentum.md`) - - A student asks the right question in office hours.
9. **Chapter 8 — Identical Particles** (`chapters/08-identical-particles.md`) - - Two electrons in helium are not "electron 1 and electron 2.
10. **Chapter 9 — Approximation Methods** (`chapters/09-approximation-methods.md`) - - Five Tools for the Problems Schrödinger Couldn't Solve.
11. **Chapter 10 — Quantum Mechanics in the Modern World** (`chapters/10-quantum-mechanics-in-the-modern-world.md`) - - The chapter moves through Entanglement, EPR, and the CHSH inequality, Lasers and stimulated emission, Semiconductors and band theory, Quantum computing, and related ideas.
